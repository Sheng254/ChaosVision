Studio Guide and Tutorial

1. 3D Camera and Navigation

Navigate and inspect the dynamical patterns from any perspective:

  • 3D Orbit / Rotate: Left-click and drag anywhere on the canvas
  • Pan View: Right-click and drag, or Shift + Drag
  • Zoom In / Out: Mouse wheel scroll or trackpad pinch
  • Recenter Camera: Click "Recenter" in the top bar or press R

2. The Dynamical Control Deck

Fine-tune the mathematical physics in real time:

  • System Parameters: Sliders dynamically adapt to the active model
  • Swarm Trajectories: Spawn parallel paths with 0.0001 initial offset
  • Color Palettes: Choose from curated bioluminescent gradients
  • Simulation Speed: Speed up or slow down integration time

3. Custom Formula Sandbox

Select "Custom Sandbox" from the dropdown to write your own equations:

  • Type formulas like sin(a * y) - cos(b * x)
  • Supports trigonometric, exponential, and algebraic functions
  • Runs with safe zero-allocation bytecode compilation at 60 FPS

4. Audio and Studio Exports

Immerse yourself and capture high-resolution media:

  • Audio: Click "Audio: ON" for soothing Zen singing bowls
  • Vector SVG: Export plotter vector line drawings
  • 4K Wallpaper: Generates a pristine 3840x2160 PNG render
  • Record Video: Live 60FPS video recording for as long as you wish
  • Share: Copies link preserving your exact parameters
